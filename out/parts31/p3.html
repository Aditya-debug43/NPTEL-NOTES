L31–L32: Planning, calming & TSM

  • Sustainable transport = least damageable to environment + doesn't impair future generations' mobility. Planning preference: accessibility > mobility; early planning = induced demand; traffic problems rose since 1970s.
  • Planning process 5 stages: goals → options → actors/stakeholders (public participation) → predict outcomes & costs (critical) → choose action (political).
  • Forecast flaws: A under-estimation (early), B over-estimation (late), C "return to normal" bias. Path dependency = locked in. Megalopolis examples: BostWash, Tokaido, Pearl River Delta, Shanghai–Nanjing.
  • Calming: vertical deflection, horizontal shift, narrowing (→ speed); closures (→ cut-through). Hump ≈ 30 km/h; bump more aggressive; table = flat top; choker = mid-block curb extension; lateral shift/chicane ≤ 35 mph; road diet 4→3 lanes (TWLTL); Seattle Rainier Ave −95% speed.
  • TSM = most productive, cost-effective use of existing services. T-turn / G-turn / Q-turn. One-way = least expensive. Van pool 7–15. Car pool = 2nd most common US work mode; HOV lane min 3 riders. Staggered hours: core time fixed; 1 SEK = 8.61 INR. Congestion pricing: London 2003 cordon, Singapore. London CPZ Mon–Fri 7–7, Sat 7–11. Delhi–Gurugram 14 km, trucks barred 8–10 AM & 5–10 PM. Exclusive bus lane: min 3 lanes, next to curb (Ahmedabad). Odd-even fails: second cheap car. Jakarta: 2 Sundays/month.

L33–L35: LCA

  • LCA = entire life cycle; 4 objectives (product improvement, decision making, environment planning, marketing); 4 stages (goal & scope → LCI → impact assessment → interpretation).
  • Boundaries: cradle-grave (linear) · cradle-cradle (closed-loop, recycling) · cradle-gate (production) · gate-grave (use+end-of-life) · gate-gate (production only) · well-to-wheel (fuels/vehicles) · EIOLCA (sector averages).
  • Scope 8 items; functional unit vs reference flow; allocation: mass / heating value (fuels) / market value. LCIA steps (EPA, 7): categories, classification, characterization, normalization, grouping, weighting, evaluating & reporting. Impact categories: global warming, acidification, eutrophication, smog, ozone depletion.
  • ISO: 165 bodies, 1947. 14040 principles · 14041 goal/scope+LCI · 14042 LCIA · 14043 interpretation · 14047 LCIA examples · 14048 data formatting · 14049 examples for 14041.
  • Types: conceptual (life cycle thinking) · simplified (screening, simplifying, reliability) · detailed (Consoli 1993: planning, screening, data collection & treatment, evaluation, improvement assessment).
  • Milan/Turin: LTZ; Milan 8.2 km², Turin 2.58 km²; baseline diesel vs ZEV; GHG −37% / −50%; PM +13% / +4%; NMVOC ≈ −50%; 50% PV → comparable; 100% solar → PM −7%; Turin all EURO 5, Milan EURO 4+5.
  • Denmark: 100% renewable 2050; wind up to 50%; 2008 fossil 84% / renewable 16% / oil 39%; transport 34%, households 29%, industry 18%; scenarios 2008, 2030, 2050 CSV/RME/BtL; functional unit = meeting energy demand; horizon 100 yr; GW 68→38→20/31/17; fossil diesel preferred except global warming (FT-biodiesel).

L36–L37: MFA

  • MFA = flows & stocks of materials in system defined in space & time (Brunner & Rechberger 2004); Input = Output + Stock; Lavoisier 1789; Greek philosophers >2000 yrs. Flow = mass/time; flux = mass/time/area. Procedure 5 steps. Classification: material (flow/substance/product) & system boundary (region/sector/firm). Lead example 200 − 150 = 50 g. Numerical: stock 1584, input 1640, output 56 t CO₂/day.
  • MFA (transparency, no impact analysis; can form LCA inventory) vs LCA (completeness) vs EIA (local project impacts).
  • Vienna: ~2 million; 2016–2050; materials in t; 12 material groups; MIT/NMIT/PT; BAU modal split 25/38/37; B car 25%, PT 45%, NMT 30%; C PT 55%, MIT <10%, NMT 35%; D NMT >45%, PT 45%; 95% motorbikes battery; tram 33%→47%; C = 70% fewer cars than B; D least material; vehicle maintenance & network operation excluded; 50% by 2030 & 100% by 2050 CO₂ target.

L38–L39: Circular economy

  • Linear = take-make-consume-throw away. CE principles: design out waste/pollution; keep products & materials in use; regenerate natural systems. Ideas: performance economy, cradle to cradle (7), biomimicry (model, measure, mentor; Benyus), industrial ecology, natural capital (4 principles), blue economy (21 principles), regenerative design (agriculture), sharing economy, cascades. Butterfly: biological (green, left) / technological (blue, right). Refurbish (no disassembly) vs remanufacture (disassembled). Stats: 2–5% GDP lost to congestion; 1/3 food wasted (USD 1 trillion); >10% hungry.
  • Transport CE: Renault 36% recycled, 85% ELV recyclable; London walking: £9.3 bn congestion by 2030, 1 million trips, £2.2 bn, £1.6 bn health; Paris logistics hotels (tricycles 180 kg); China CNY 12.6 trillion (36%), 49% (2030)/71% (2040); São Paulo 25%→70% (2015→2025), 30% funds; India scrappage 2021-22, 4–6% scrap value; Kera (Espoo): 3 scenarios, S3 energy halved, emissions 10% of S1.
