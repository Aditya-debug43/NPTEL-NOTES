Important Distinctions to Remember

  • Draisienne 1817 (first two-wheeler, wood, foot-paddling) ≠ Velocipede 1860s (Michaux, pedalling cranks on front axle, "boneshaker") ≠ Penny-farthing 1883 ≠ Rover Safety (chain drive, wheels same size, forerunner of modern bikes).
  • Cheshunt 1825 = first monorail (horse-drawn) vs 1876 = first steam-driven monorail.
  • Cugnot 1769 = first true automobile (steam) vs Benz 1885 = first gasoline car vs Reitwagen 1885 = first true motorcycle.
  • Cayley 1853 = first successful manned flight (glider) vs Wright glider 1902 (first fully controllable) vs Wright Flyer 1905 (first practical) vs Vickers Vimy 1919 (first nonstop transatlantic).
  • Concorde (first supersonic passenger commercial airplane, UK+France) vs Tu-144 (first passenger aircraft to break sound barrier, June 5 1969).
  • Viking: war ship = langskip; merchant ship = knorr. Airships use hydrogen.

Definitions L2·s3

  • Transportation system: "the combination of elements and their interactions, which produce the demand for travel within a given area and the supply of transportation services to satisfy this demand." (Ennio Cascetta, 2001)
  • Transport modes: the various networks used to transport people and/or goods; include Motorized and Non-Motorized Transport (NMT) modes.

Multimodal transport L2·s33–35

  • Combining multiple modes to transport people/goods effectively from source to destination; also called "Combined transport" or "Intermodal transport".
  • Ensures seamless travel by: Vehicle mode integration (car, BRTS, metro etc.); Service integration (private & public modes); Infrastructure integration (bus & metro stations, parking facilities etc.).
  • Concept (s34): Home → Access modes (walking, bicycle/rickshaws, 2 wheeler, bus) → transfer stations (Car/Bus/Metro/BRTS/MRTS) → Egress modes (walking, bicycle/rickshaws, 2 wheeler, bus) → Office.
  • Challenges (s35): poor infrastructure facilities; intermodal linkage at regional and local levels; current aged transport modes; enhance ICT; modernization of public passenger terminals; role of Government to facilitate MMT.

Conclusions L2·s36

  • Transportation systems include all modes (road, rail, air, waterway, pipeline and cable) with infrastructure.
  • Efficient system = efficient policy measures + good infrastructure + integrated modes + integration with ICT to tackle congestion, pollution, negative externalities. Sustainable solutions = the future.

Important Distinctions to Remember

  • Efficiency factors (11, Kadiyali) vs Development factors (5 groups × 4 scales, Rodrigue) vs Components (Sussman).
  • Frequency (how often) vs Regularity (punctuality/adhering to schedule) vs Adequacy (capacity).
  • Stakeholders are NOT customers or suppliers; Financial community = equity + debt.
  • Competition: intra-modal vs inter-modal (rail vs truck).
