Definitions & lists

  • Transportation system = elements + interactions producing demand for travel and supply of transport services (Cascetta 2001). Multimodal = Combined/Intermodal transport; integration of vehicle mode, service, infrastructure.
  • 11 efficiency factors: Speed, Safety, Adequacy, Frequency, Regularity, Integration, Responsibilities, Comfort, Cheapness, Fuel efficiency, Employment generation.
  • Development factors: Environmental, Historical, Technological, Political, Economic × Local, Regional, National/Transnational, Global.
  • Sussman components: Government, Competition, Supply industry, Stakeholders, Financial community (+ customer, general public).
  • Sustainable development (Brundtland 1987) – needs of present without compromising future generations. Pillars: Social, Environmental, Economic. Principles: environmentally bearable, socially equitable, economically viable. Stages: before 1972 / 1972–1987 / after 1987.
  • Sustainable transport (UNDESA 2014) – safe, affordable, accessible, efficient, resilient + minimizing carbon/other emissions. SDGs: 2, 3, 7, 9, 11, 12, 13. "5 Is and 5 Cs".
  • GWP converts gases to CO2 equivalents; radiative forcing = heating effect of GHGs.
  • Impact types (L6): direct / indirect (higher consequence) / cumulative.
  • Health (L7): 3 broad ways – accidents, pollution, infectious diseases. Strategies: traffic calming, active transport, public transit, pricing reforms, mobility management marketing, smart growth. DPSEEA.
  • Flora/fauna (L8): Direct 8 + Indirect 4; four main effects; mitigation crossing structures by animal size.
  • Noise (L10): Subjective / Behavioral / Physiological; Leq, A-weighting, L10>L50>L90; transmission vs insertion loss.
